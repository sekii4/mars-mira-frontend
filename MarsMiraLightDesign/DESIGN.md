## Brand & Style

This design system is tailored for an approachable, inspiring walking and hiking companion. Balancing natural exploration with mindful movement, the interface conveys optimism, effortless outdoor clarity, and calm encouragement. It avoids the intimidating tactical aesthetic of extreme mountaineering tools, replacing it with an inviting, tactile lightness that celebrates neighborhood morning walks as warmly as alpine day treks.

### Aesthetics & Philosophy
- **Gentle Tactility:** Soft, organic rounded surfaces, subtle tint-based elevations, and generous, breathable margins that make data feel weightless.
- **Sunlit Clarity:** Off-white natural surfaces, soft mint tints, and fresh emerald accents create an open, outdoorsy atmosphere with high legibility under daylight.
- **Friendly Precision:** Clean typography and rounded badges present pace, elevation, and mileage as achievable milestones rather than punishing workout metrics.
- **Playful Respect:** Playful and uplifting without being childish; dependable, respectful of safety, and structured for quick thumb-reach interactions.

### Target Audience
Everyday walkers, recreational day-hikers, weekend trail wanderers, and wellness-focused explorers seeking an uncluttered, cheerful, and readable interface to record and cherish outdoor moments.

## Colors

The color palette centers on radiant outdoor light, warm natural air, and lush foliage. The system departs decisively from dark, tactical palettes, utilizing a soft-white baseline accented with vibrant emerald and calming sage.

### Chromatic Roles
- **Primary (`#10B981`):** Vivid emerald green used for key action triggers, live tracking polylines, recorded milestones, active progress indicators, and primary navigation states.
- **Secondary (`#6EE7B7`):** Fresh mint highlight used for interactive states, secondary progression tracks, and soft interactive fills.
- **Tertiary (`#84A98C`):** Subdued organic sage used for subtle environmental icons, topography hints, and balanced nature accents.
- **Neutral (`#F8FAF8`):** Off-white base canvas with a warm, natural undertone that reduces glare under direct sunlight compared to harsh `#FFFFFF`.
- **Text Main (`#1E293B`):** Deep slate providing crisp, high-contrast legibility across all off-white and tinted surfaces.
- **Warm Pastel Badges:** Soft peach, butter, sky, and lavender tones for collectible trail badges, milestone celebrations, and elevation category tags.

## Typography

The typography uses Plus Jakarta Sans across all layers. Its rounded humanist geometry, generous counter-forms, and wide open apertures create an approachable, lively personality while retaining top-tier legibility when viewed in motion on vibrating handsets.

### Scaling & Roles
- **Headlines:** Rounded, bold headers provide upbeat hierarchy for route destinations, achievement banners, and daily goal check-ins.
- **Body:** Open line heights and balanced weights guarantee easy reading of trail descriptions, terrain advice, and community updates.
- **Metrics & Labels:** High-weight, tightly kerned numerals bring dynamic satisfaction to live stats (kilometers walked, steps taken, elevation gain), keeping live stats glanceable without the cold sterility of traditional monospaced fonts.

## Layout & Spacing

A fluid grid model grounded in an 8pt spatial cadence with 4pt micro-steps gives this design system generous whitespace, preventing visual fatigue during active outdoor activities.

### Layout Model & Rhythm
- **Breathing Room:** Outer viewports utilize a minimum padding of 20px (`gutter-mobile`) to keep actionable controls safely separated from hardware screen edges and phone cases.
- **Vertical Breathing:** Section groupings preserve at least 24px–32px of negative space, framing cards as comfortable floating pods rather than tightly packed data matrices.
- **Breakpoints:**
  - **Mobile (<640px):** Single-column stacked stream. Sticky bottom-anchored control capsules for primary walk controls within comfortable thumb range.
  - **Tablet (640px–1024px):** 6-column fluid grid. Map viewport sits persistently beside telemetry summaries and interactive milestone feeds.
  - **Desktop / Web Preview (>1024px):** 12-column layout maxing out at 1200px centered width, presenting trail exploration maps flanked by itinerary cards and activity recaps.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual depth avoids heavy, sharp drop shadows. Instead, it relies on soft, atmospheric ambient diffusion and clean surface tone stepping to create friendly, float-like layers.

### Tonal Hierarchy
- **Canvas (`#F8FAF8`):** Gentle, warm off-white foundation giving brightness without glare.
- **Card Surface (`#FFFFFF`):** Pure white raised cards that separate naturally from the canvas.
- **Tinted Layer (`#ECFDF5`):** Soft mint pods used for highlighted activity summaries, active route cards, and goal completion containers.

### Shadows & Borders
- **Ambient Glow Shadow:** Primary elevation uses multi-layered, ultra-soft shadows tinted with neutral slate: `0 4px 20px -2px rgba(30, 41, 59, 0.05), 0 1px 3px 0 rgba(30, 41, 59, 0.03)`.
- **Floating Action Shadow:** Elevated tracking pills and primary buttons use emerald-tinted diffusion: `0 8px 24px -4px rgba(16, 185, 129, 0.28)`.
- **Soft Border Definition:** All cards incorporate a subtle 1px border (`#E2E8F0` at 60% opacity or `#10B981` at 12% on mint panels) ensuring clean structural boundaries even under bright sunlight.

## Shapes

The shape language is smooth, friendly, and organic (`roundedness: 2`), reflecting the natural curves of trails and walking paths.

- **Standard Cards & Pods:** 16px (`1rem` / `rounded-lg`) corner radii create a cuddly, accessible feel.
- **Modals & Overlays:** Generous 24px (`1.5rem` / `rounded-xl`) radii for bottom sheets and popup summaries.
- **Interactive Controls:** Buttons, chips, search inputs, and status badges utilize full pill geometry (`9999px`), ensuring they look soft, welcoming, and tactile to tap.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary ("Start Walk", "Save Trail"):** Fully rounded pill (`border-radius: 9999px`), solid `#10B981` fill, pure white bold text, with emerald ambient shadow (`rgba(16, 185, 129, 0.28)`). Height: 52px for primary walk triggers.
- **Secondary ("Pause", "View Elevation"):** Pill shape, `#ECFDF5` background with `#10B981` bold text, no shadow.
- **Ghost / Tertiary:** Transparent background, slate text (`#1E293B`), active state reveals a soft `#F1F5F9` circular touch wash.

### Cards & Stat Pods
- Background of `#FFFFFF`, rounded 16px (`rounded-lg`), subtle 1px `#E2E8F0` border, and soft ambient shadow.
- Inner layout gives generous 20px internal padding.
- Metric values stand out with `label-metric-xl` in `#1E293B`, accompanied by tiny, uppercase metadata labels in `#64748B`.

### Badges & Milestone Chips
- Full pill shape with internal padding `4px 12px`.
- Friendly pastel palettes:
  - Trail Peak: Peach background (`#FFEDD5`) with `#9A3412` text.
  - Hydration / Lake: Sky background (`#E0F2FE`) with `#0369A1` text.
  - Daily Streak: Butter background (`#FEF3C7`) with `#92400E` text.
  - Woodland / Flora: Mint background (`#ECFDF5`) with `#047857` text.

### Interactive Form Inputs
- Rounded pill search bars and profile inputs with `#FFFFFF` background, inset padding of 16px, and `#E2E8F0` borders.
- Focus state activates a 2px `#10B981` border with an ultra-soft mint outer glow (`0 0 0 4px rgba(16, 185, 129, 0.15)`).

### Toggle Controls, Checkboxes & Radios
- **Switches:** Pill tracks with a 4px inner margin; unchecked state is `#E2E8F0`, checked state transitions to `#10B981` with a smooth white circular thumb.
- **Checkboxes:** 22px rounded squares (`6px` radius); active state filled with `#10B981` containing a crisp white rounded checkmark.

### Route Milestones & Trail Progress Bars
- Smooth horizontal bar with 8px radius. Background tracks use `#ECFDF5`, while active filled progress utilizes a gradient from `#6EE7B7` to `#10B981`.
- Step markers along the bar use cute circular avatars or icon stamps.
